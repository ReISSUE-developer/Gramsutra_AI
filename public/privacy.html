Information processed

  • Local profile and app preferences. Name, phone, village, role and language in the basic profile are stored on this device. The optional Community account stores your email, display name, village, role and language in Supabase; signed-in members may discover profile details.
  • Location and weather. Location is requested only after you tap a location/weather control. Coordinates are sent to the GramSutra server and Open-Meteo to fetch a forecast. The app may retain the last location on your device to remember your map view, but does not automatically send it for weather requests.
  • Field boundary and satellite health. Field polygon coordinates are stored in browser local storage. When you request satellite analysis, the polygon is sent to the GramSutra server and Sentinel Hub to fetch Sentinel-2 vegetation imagery. A satellite basemap is served by Esri. The imagery and analysis may be incomplete or cloud affected.
  • Crop photos. Photos you choose for AI crop review are sent to the GramSutra server and then Google Gemini for visual analysis. The app does not intentionally persist the photo on its server. Avoid uploading people, documents, exact addresses or sensitive images.
  • Pragati AI messages. Text and relevant app context are forwarded by the server to Google Gemini to generate replies. The app server does not intentionally save chat history. Gemini API free-tier content may be used by Google to improve its products; avoid sending sensitive personal data unless you have reviewed the applicable Google terms and chosen an appropriate paid service tier.
  • Marketplace and inquiries. Signed-in marketplace listings and private buyer/seller inquiries are stored in Supabase. Listings may be visible to other signed-in members. Inquiries are visible to the sender and the listing owner.
  • Farm calendar. Tasks are stored locally for offline access. When signed in and online, they are synchronized to your private Supabase account.
  • Community, messaging and calls. Profiles, connection requests and private messages are stored in Supabase and restricted by database access policies. Audio/video calls use your microphone and optional camera only after permission. Signaling is exchanged through Supabase Realtime; media may travel peer-to-peer or through configured TURN relays. Calls are not recorded by this app.
  • Voice controls. Speech recognition and speech synthesis use browser/device capabilities. Speech recognition may be processed by the browser or operating-system provider; refer to that provider's privacy terms.

Providers and external services

Depending on the feature used, data may be processed by Supabase, Google Gemini, Open-Meteo, data.gov.in, Sentinel Hub, Esri, the device/browser speech service and the configured TURN provider. The app owner must add provider agreements and disclosures appropriate to their deployment.

Retention and controls

Local data can be cleared through browser/app storage settings. Cloud profile, messages, listings and tasks remain in Supabase until deleted according to the operator's retention and account-deletion process. Add a clear in-app account deletion flow before public launch.

Location, photos and account deletion

With your permission, the app uses your phone’s location to show local weather and to place your field on a map. Location is sent to our server only to fetch weather and satellite data and is not stored. Crop photos you choose to analyse are sent to Google’s Gemini service to produce suggestions. If you create a community account, you can delete it permanently from inside the app (Community, Delete my account), which removes your account and profile.

Important notice

GramSutra provides decision-support information, not certified agronomic, medical, financial or legal advice. AI crop observations and NDVI are not confirmed diagnoses. Confirm high-impact decisions with a local agriculture officer or KVK. Scheme eligibility and deadlines must be checked with official authorities. Map boundaries do not establish legal ownership.
